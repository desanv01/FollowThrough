# Prior art and contribution boundaries

Research reviewed in planning on 6 November 2026; no first-of-kind claim.

- [LoopsBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00267): sustained coding loops and regression obligations.
- [InterventionBench](https://github.com/jhn25/InterventionBench): revised-goal-from-start versus mid-task revision controls.
- [HSC-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36523): inherited task state and continuation.
- [OrchestraBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05263): controlled orchestration faults/recovery.
- [Backcheck](https://github.com/VectorInstitute/backcheck): claims checked against execution evidence.

Proposed contribution: original small repair scenarios measuring completion under standing delegation rules and current evidence, plus a controlled progress-record intervention. Originality lies in the actual tasks, measurements and results, not merely CLI use, multi-agent roles or fault injection. No upstream dataset copied.
